What this site doesn't collect

  • This site doesn't use cookies.
  • There's no analytics, no ads and no third-party tracking scripts.
  • There are no input forms.

What is stored in your browser

Only when you press the light/dark toggle, your choice (light or dark) is saved in your browser's localStorage so the site looks the same next time. It is never sent anywhere. Clearing it returns the site to following your device's setting.

What is handled to deliver the site

This site is served by Cloudflare. To deliver it, Cloudflare handles access information such as your IP address and browser type, under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

External sites

Links to GitHub, Zenn, Misskey, the VS Code Marketplace and other sites are governed by each site's own privacy policy.
